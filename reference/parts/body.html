Vipulasena Pathirajage

Former Director of Science Education
Ministry of Education, Sri Lanka

  • BSc
  • PGDE
  • MA

I completed my first degree at the University of Kelaniya and soon afterwards joined the Sri Lanka Education Administrative Service. My first appointment was as Principal of a national school. I was then appointed Assistant Director of Science Education for the North Central Province, and in 1999 I became Zonal Director of Education, Kekirawa. I later served as Deputy Commissioner in the Department of Examinations, and in 2004 I was appointed Director of Education Quality Assurance. My final appointment was as Director of Science Education.

Having worked in so many parts of the education system, I was able to make a significant contribution to science education in Sri Lanka, including curricula, development programmes and textbooks. I also conducted many teacher education workshops across the country. Since retiring, I have felt it would be worthwhile to share these experiences with the public, and this blog is where I hope to do that.
